# 60-Second Demo Video Script

**Scene 1 — Hook (0-10s)**
- Title card: “Never Miss a Call Again.”
- Voiceover: “Meet Cascade Flow Plumbing’s new bilingual voice assistant. Every call becomes a booked job.”

**Scene 2 — Caller Experience (10-30s)**
- Screen recording of telephony harness visualizer or waveform.
- Voiceover: “When a homeowner calls, the assistant greets them in English or Spanish, transcribes the conversation, and understands intent in real time.”
- Highlight captions: “Real-time STT → LLM → TTS pipeline.”

**Scene 3 — Booking Confirmation (30-45s)**
- Cut to scheduler response overlay showing travel-aware slot selection.
- Voiceover: “It checks the calendar, accounts for travel buffers, and books instantly.”
- Display SMS template snippet confirming the appointment.

**Scene 4 — Dashboard + Traceability (45-55s)**
- Show dashboard leads page with transcript and summary.
- Voiceover: “Staff can review transcripts, summaries, and notifications inside the dashboard—complete with trace IDs and sanitized PII.”

**Scene 5 — Call-to-Action (55-60s)**
- Return to landing page call-to-action.
- Voiceover: “Launch your own assistant in under 15 minutes. Visit contractorva.com to book a pilot.”

> Tip: Record the narration separately, then mix with background music at -20 dB for polish. Export to MP4 (1080p, 60 seconds) and upload to the CMS, linking it from the landing page.
