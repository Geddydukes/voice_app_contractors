Cascade Flow Plumbing Installation Overview
==========================================

Scope of Work
-------------
- Standard water heater replacement (40–50 gallon)
- Optional tankless system installation
- Emergency services for burst pipes and leaks

Preparation Checklist
---------------------
1. Confirm service address within 30-mile radius of downtown Portland.
2. Verify customer availability window and access instructions.
3. Shut off water and power supply prior to technician arrival.

Installation Steps
------------------
1. Drain and disconnect existing unit, hauling away old equipment.
2. Install new connections with dielectric unions and updated venting.
3. Test system for leaks, relight pilot or configure electrical connections.
4. Review maintenance guide with homeowner and collect final payment.

Safety & Compliance
-------------------
- All work follows Oregon plumbing code and manufacturer requirements.
- Technicians wear PPE and protect work areas with drop cloths.
- Permit coordination handled by Cascade Flow Plumbing scheduling team.

Customer Follow-Up
------------------
- Provide digital receipt and warranty documentation via email within 24 hours.
- Send SMS check-in 48 hours post-install to confirm satisfaction.
- Offer maintenance plan enrollment with annual inspection reminder.
